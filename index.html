⚠️ Before you buy

If you are flying to Japan specifically to buy a helmet, check these first.

🌍 Certification

Japan-market specifications and certifications may differ from those in your home market. Check whether the helmet is legal for road use where you live.

🪖 Fit

Head circumference alone does not determine fit. Try the actual model and have the fit checked at a dealer.

🛡️ Genuine product

Prefer SHOEI Galleries or authorized dealers. Be cautious with marketplace listings and counterfeit products.

🧳 Before the flight

Confirm model, size/color, store opening hours, fitting/PFS service, payment methods and tax-free eligibility before travelling.

Important: This guide helps you plan a purchase trip. It does not guarantee stock or legal eligibility in your home country.

🇯🇵 Why buy a SHOEI in Japan?

For an overseas rider, the useful question is not only "Can I buy it here?" but "Is there a reason to make the trip?"

🇯🇵 Japan-limited models

Some models and graphics are sold through Japan-specific channels. X-Fifteen Carbon is a good example: SHOEI describes sales through SHOEI Gallery stores, excluding the Online Store, plus selected SHOEI TECHNICAL SHOP locations.

🎨 Japan-market graphics

Japan's lineup can include graphics and editions that are difficult to find in other markets. Use the Japan name and international name together to identify the model you are looking for.

🎯 Try before you fly home

SHOEI Galleries let you see and try current helmets. Ask about fitting and PFS where available, especially if you are travelling specifically for a new helmet.

🔎 Verify the difference

Japan availability does not automatically mean the helmet has the same certification, specification, or road legality as a helmet sold in your home country. Check before purchase.

🏪 X-Fifteen family: Gallery handling is inherited across X-Fifteen graphics/derivatives. A ○ means the Gallery handles the X-Fifteen family, not that a specific graphic or size is physically in stock.
