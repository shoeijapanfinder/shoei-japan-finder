SHOEI Japan Finder

A practical guide for overseas riders who want to buy a SHOEI helmet in Japan.

About

This site helps overseas riders plan a SHOEI helmet shopping trip in Japan.

It covers:

• Japanese-market and limited models
• Japanese and international model names
• Prices and product information
• SHOEI Gallery locations
• Fitting and P.F.S. information
• Travel planning tips
• Links to official SHOEI information

Important

Store listings do not guarantee physical stock of a specific model, graphic, colour, or size.

Prices, product lineups, store information, services, and availability can change. Please confirm important details with SHOEI or the relevant store before travelling.

For helmets purchased in Japan, travellers should also confirm that the helmet’s certification and specifications are suitable and legal for road use in their home country.

Disclaimer

Always use official SHOEI information as the final source for purchase decisions.
