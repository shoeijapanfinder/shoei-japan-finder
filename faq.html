🪖 Fit, sizing & P.F.S.

The safest way to choose a Japan-market SHOEI is to use the actual helmet, not a size-conversion chart alone.

Is a Japan-market SHOEI the same size as my US/EU helmet?

Do not assume that a letter size such as M or L will fit identically across markets or models. Head circumference is only one part of fit. The practical approach is to try the actual Japan-market model and have the fit checked by a dealer.

For travelers, this is one reason a SHOEI Gallery or a dealer offering fitting services can be more useful than ordering a helmet online.

Does Japan-market SHOEI fit differently from US/EU models?

Do not assume that Japan-market and overseas versions will fit identically just because the size label is the same. Fit can vary by model, shell/interior configuration and head shape. The official SHOEI sizing guidance also says head circumference alone cannot determine the correct size and recommends trying the desired model at a dealer.

For a trip purchase, treat your measured circumference as a starting point. Try the actual helmet and ask about P.F.S. rather than relying on a size-conversion chart.

SHOEI official sizing guidance ↗

What is P.F.S.?

P.F.S. (Personal Fitting System) is SHOEI's fitting service for adjusting the helmet to the rider. SHOEI Galleries state that P.F.S. is available there on an ongoing basis.

Ask the store what fitting service is available for the exact helmet you intend to buy. Availability and fitting details can vary by store and product.

Should I choose my size before I travel?

Use your head circumference as a starting point, but treat it as a starting point only. If the purchase is the main purpose of your trip, leave enough time for trying the helmet and fitting it before you fly home.

Trip-planning rule: model → size → color → fitting/P.F.S. → store → pickup. Confirm these in that order when possible.

⚠️ Certification & road legality

Can I legally use a Japan-market SHOEI in my country?

There is no single worldwide answer. Japan-market helmets can have different certification markings, specifications and approval requirements from helmets sold in your home market.

Before buying, check the road-helmet requirements of the country or state/province where you will actually ride. If your local law or insurer requires a particular certification, confirm that the Japan-market helmet satisfies that requirement.

This site is a travel and shopping guide, not legal advice.

Does a SHOEI helmet being genuine mean it is legal everywhere?

No. Authenticity and legal approval are different questions. A genuine SHOEI can still have a market-specific certification that does not meet a particular local requirement.

What should I check before paying?
  • The exact Japan-market model and specification.
  • The certification markings on the helmet.
  • Your local road-use requirements.
  • Any insurer or competition rules that apply to your use.
Important: Do not rely on the fact that another rider uses the same helmet in your country. Regulations can differ by country, state/province and use case.

SHOEI Gallery or RICOLAND/NAPS/Nankai Parts: which should I choose?

There is no universal answer. A SHOEI Gallery is the official SHOEI showroom route and is useful when you want to see current helmets and ask about fitting/P.F.S. Authorized motorcycle retailers can be useful when you want to compare brands, check retailer-specific services, or ask about tax-free shopping.

  • SHOEI Gallery: official SHOEI location, fitting/P.F.S. support, but do not assume tax-free treatment or physical stock.
  • RICOLAND / NAPS / Nankai Parts: authorized retailer options; services such as P.F.S. and tax-free shopping vary by individual store.

For a trip built around one exact helmet, compare the exact store's model handling, fitting service, tax-free policy and ability to secure your size/color before travelling.

Can overseas riders create mySHOEI before arriving in Japan?

SHOEI's current Gallery workflow uses mySHOEI for visit reservations. The registration form uses Japanese-style address fields and full-width kana fields, which can be inconvenient for overseas visitors.

The Finder does not state that overseas visitors are prohibited from registering. If registration does not work from abroad, contact SHOEI before travelling rather than assuming that a reservation or helmet is secured.

Remember that a Gallery visit reservation is not the same thing as reserving a specific model, color or size.

Does a Gallery visit reservation reserve my helmet?

No. SHOEI's Gallery information says reservations receive priority guidance, but the product reservation and physical stock are separate questions. SHOEI's stock guidance specifically recommends checking desired-product stock before making a showroom reservation.

If your trip depends on one exact model, color or size, contact the Gallery before travelling and confirm what can actually be secured.

Can I get P.F.S. at every motorcycle shop?

No. P.F.S. availability depends on the store and service setup. SHOEI Galleries advertise P.F.S., while other retailers should be checked individually.

Is SHOEI Gallery always the cheapest option?

Not necessarily. Price, tax treatment, retailer promotions and available services can differ. If price is important, compare the final out-of-pocket cost rather than only the displayed tax-included price.

Does “○” on the Finder mean the helmet is in stock?

No. A ○ means the official SHOEI store locator lists that model as handled by the store. It is not live physical inventory and does not guarantee your color or size is on the shelf.

If the helmet is the reason for your trip, confirm the exact model, graphic and size with the store before travelling.

✈️ Buying the helmet and taking it home

Can I buy a helmet on the first day of my trip?

Yes, but do not build your entire plan around finding a specific size/color without confirmation. Limited graphics and popular sizes can disappear from individual stores.

If the helmet is the main purpose of the trip, consider visiting the relevant store early in your itinerary so you have time to solve a sizing or stock problem.

Can I buy tax-free in Japan?

Tax-free eligibility and procedures depend on the retailer, your traveler status and the current Japanese tax-free rules. Do not assume that every SHOEI retailer, including official locations, handles tax-free purchases in the same way.

SHOEI Gallery: this Finder does not claim a universal Gallery-wide tax-free policy. If tax-free treatment matters to your budget, confirm the exact Gallery or retailer before travelling. Policies can change.

Confirm the store's current policy before travelling and bring the documents required for tax-free shopping.

Can I carry the helmet onto an airplane?

Airline baggage rules vary. Check your airline's current cabin-baggage size/weight rules and ask whether the helmet can be carried as part of your cabin baggage allowance. Do not rely on another airline's policy.

If space is tight, travellers sometimes choose to discard or collapse the retail box and carry the helmet in a helmet bag, but that is a traveller workaround, not an airline rule. Check the airline before your purchase trip.

Can the store order my size for pickup later in Japan?

Possibly, but do not assume it. SHOEI says stock and lead times for authorized retailers should be confirmed with the retailer itself. If your itinerary depends on a later pickup, ask the exact store whether they can order the model/color/size and hold it for you.

SHOEI official stock/lead-time guidance ↗

What if the store cannot provide my size?

Ask whether another branch has the size, whether the retailer can order it, and whether pickup can be arranged before you leave Japan. If the helmet is a limited edition, ask about the order period and expected shipping date.
